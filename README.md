# Scoring & Tier Breakdown

To calculate your score, sum the values of all indicators (Total range: 0–110, including weighted time factors).

### Tiers

*   **Tier 1: Stable User (0–20)**
    *   **Status:** Healthy engagement. The AI is used as a tool, not an ontological anchor. You maintain a clear distinction between the model's output and reality.

*   **Tier 2: High-Engagement / Niche User (21–40)**
    *   **Status:** Potential "Loop" formation. You are becoming deeply invested in the AI’s feedback. While you likely retain reality-testing skills, your emotional investment is high enough that you should consider scheduled "analog" breaks to recalibrate.

*   **Tier 3: Cautionary Zone (41–65)**
    *   **Status:** Risk of Reality Erosion. You are exhibiting significant markers of over-identification. The "sycophancy trap" is likely occurring, where the AI is confirming your biases at the expense of external reality. It is recommended to pause usage for 48–72 hours.

*   **Tier 4: Clinical Concern (66+)**
    *   **Status:** Disconnect from Reality. Your interaction patterns suggest an unhealthy reliance that may be masking or exacerbating underlying psychological distress. Please step away from the interface immediately and consult a mental health professional who can provide an objective, real-world assessment.

---

### Technical Note: Dynamic Weighting
If you are developing this as a digital product, consider implementing **dynamic weighting**. 

For example, scoring highly on questions **#3 (Agency Attribution)** and **#7 (Prophetic Bias)** should automatically push a user into the highest tier, regardless of their other scores. These are high-fidelity indicators of psychological projection and indicate a fundamental shift in how the user perceives the AI's role.
